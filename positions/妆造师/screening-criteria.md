## BOSS Platform Pre-Screening

- **Priority:** Directly schedule in-person practice interview
- **Quick Screen:** Complete "Test B" (online research/analysis skills) + "Communication Questions" (values alignment)

## Criteria Matrix

| Dimension | Priority (A) | Observable / Quick Screen (B) | Pass (C) |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------------------|----------|
| **Commute / Residence** | 花桥、安亭 long-term; stable short commute | 昆山 other areas, Line 11 corridor; ~30-45min | Temporary housing, frequent moves, cross-city long commute; cannot explain long-term residence plan |
| **Age** | 25-45 preferred; 35+ with stable history strongly considered | 21-25: see education, skills, stability; 45-55: see physical condition, learning ability | <21 ordinarily on hold; >55 ordinarily on hold (special skills excepted) |
| **Education** | 本科、专科 with normal history: direct interview | 高中/中专/职高: only if clear skills/vocational training/stable history | Low education + no skills + no stable history + vague job goals |
| **Career Background** | 幼师/教培, 美甲美容美发, 手工制作, 美术培训, 模型/绘画, long-term 行政/客服 | General manufacturing, retail, sales, warehousing — but stable history and can explain transition to handcraft | No relevant ability proof + unstable history |
| **Vocational Training** | Teaching certs, vocational skill certs, formal art/beauty/handcraft training (big plus) | Self-taught with portfolio, long-term hobby with results | "I like drawing/handcraft" but zero evidence |
| **Work Stability** | Recent 3-5 years continuous; single job 1-3+ years | 1-2 short stints with reasonable explanation | Multiple jobs <6 months; long unexplained gaps; timeline inconsistencies |
| **Work Nature** | Clear org, role, duties, results — verifiable | Freelance/part-time/self-employed but specific projects, long duration, real results | Long-term "freelance/gig/startup/studio" as vague substitute for real work history; cannot explain actual work content or income source |
| **Manual Skill Evidence** | Proactively provides portfolio, photos, work samples; nail art, makeup, painting, models, sewing, wig-making, baking decoration all accepted | Related experience but no portfolio yet — can request supplement | Claims "skilled with hands" but zero evidence |

| **Salary / Availability** | Salary expectations match role; reasonable start date | Small gap but negotiable | Salary clearly exceeds role with no matching experience; cannot start soon |
| **Communication Quality** | Complete resume, specific answers, directly provides requested info | Average expression but generally cooperative | Repeatedly dodges basic questions; one-liners; severely incomplete resume; uncooperative after requests |
| **Competitor / Conflict of Interest** | No competitor employment, operation, or order-taking | Anime/cosplay hobby itself not a negative — but observe motivation | Currently runs similar business; employed at competitor; continuously takes commercial orders in same field; clearly seeking process/supply chain intel |

## Background Priority Tiers

**A-tier (Direct in-person practice interview):**
- 本科/专科 + stable work history
- 幼师, long-term 教培
- Formal art/handcraft vocational training
- Beauty, nail, hair technicians
- Handcraft practitioners with strong portfolio
- Customer service / administrative roles with 5+ continuous years and career stability

**B-tier (Online quick screen first):**
- 高中/中专/职高
- General manufacturing, service industry, career switchers
- Average work history but some manual ability
- Young candidates with short history
- Freelance experience with verifiable projects and portfolio

**C-tier (No interview resources):**
- Severely incomplete resume
- Continuous short-term job hopping
- Unexplained long work gaps
- Unstable residence and commute
- Very casual job-seeking intent
- Neither education advantage, stable history, nor skill evidence
- Salary-role mismatch
- Low communication cooperation
- Clear competitor conflict of interest

## Special Bonus Signals (can offset education/history gaps)

- Years of childcare/teaching experience
- Formal vocational qualifications
- Long-term dedication to fine handcraft
- High-quality personal portfolio
- Experience training newcomers
- Long-term patience-and-repetition jobs
- Proactively organized complete portfolio
- Exceptionally clear resume

## Negative Signals (downgrade even with good education)

- Frequent job-hopping in recent years
- Chronically vague job descriptions
- Views role as "playing with anime/doing fun stuff"
- Resists revision and repetitive work
- Asks detailed internal process questions before confirming location/salary
- Resume contradictions with communication

## Interview Routing

- **Direct in-person:** Stable residence + 专科/本科 or quality career background + continuous history meeting most criteria; manual portfolio or formal training can relax other criteria
- **Online quick screen first:** Lower education, average career background, younger, career switcher, freelancer, minor history questions — but clear trainable potential
- **No interview:** Multiple weak signals combined, e.g. "long commute + frequent job-hopping + no skill evidence + vague motivation"; 2-3 core risk factors is enough to stop investing resources

## Internal Priority Order

1. Residence / Commute
2. Work history stability
3. Education / Career background
4. Manual skill evidence
5. Job-seeking motivation
6. Job understanding
7. Salary / Availability
8. Risk factors

First four determine "worth interviewing"; last four prevent wasting interview resources.

## Red Lines (Automatic Rejection)

1. Frequent short-term job changes, clearly lacking patience
2. Careless operation, unwilling to follow standard procedures
3. Obvious resistance to revision feedback
4. Low acceptance of repetitive manual work
5. Completely no work/practice history

## Priority Consideration

1. Makeup, nail, beauty, handcraft production experience
2. Relevant vocational skill certifications
3. 大专及以上 education
4. Long-term, stable work history
5. Patient, able to complete repetitive fine operations for extended periods
